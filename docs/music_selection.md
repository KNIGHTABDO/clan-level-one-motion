## 6. Audio Engineering & Motion Graphics Synchronization Blueprint

To ensure seamless implementation in Remotion (`kit/src/`) and platform optimization for TikTok, Kick, and YouTube Shorts:

### 6.1 Master Bus Loudness & Dynamics
- **Integrated Loudness Target:** `-13.0 to -14.0 LUFS` with a maximum **True Peak limit of -1.0 dBTP**.
- **Reason:** TikTok normalizes audio to approximately -14 LUFS; YouTube Shorts targets -14 LUFS. Delivering at -8 LUFS causes platform limiters to squash sub-bass transients.
- **Remotion Audio Node:**
  ```tsx
  import { Audio, staticFile } from "remotion";
  
  // Example for Track #1 (21 Savage - redrum)
  <Audio
    src={staticFile("audio/redrum.mp3")}
    startFrom={0}
    endAt={60 * 15} // 15-second opening sequence
    volume={(f) => (f < 15 ? f / 15 : 1)} // 0.25s linear fade-in
  />
  ```

### 6.2 Car Engine SFX & Dialogue Ducking
When layering Ab2dy's Dodge Challenger Hellcat or streamer dialogue from Kick VODs:
- **Hellcat V8 Cold Start:** High-pass filter at 85 Hz (let the 30–80 Hz sub-bass remain clear for the music's 808s).
- **Supercharger Whine (2–4 kHz):** Apply a narrow -2.5 dB notch filter on the music track between 2.5 kHz and 4 kHz so the engine whine cuts through without clashing with synths.
- **Streamer Dialogue:** Sidechain duck the music track by **-3.5 dB** during spoken lines, releasing back to 0 dB 100ms before the drop.
